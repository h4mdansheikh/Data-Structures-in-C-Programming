5. The rules behind the animation

OperandLetters and digits go straight into the postfix output.
Opening parenthesis (Push it as a boundary marker.
Closing parenthesis )Pop operators until (, then discard the parenthesis.
OperatorPop higher-precedence operators first. For equal precedence, pop first when the current operator is left-associative.
End of inputPop remaining operators, then append the null terminator \\0.
Associativity^ is right-associative, so A^B^C becomes ABC^^.
